# Social Media Strategy — Astra AI

## 1. Product Name & Category
**Product:** Astra AI
**Category:** Personal AI Assistant & Domain-Specific Knowledge Base

## 2. Target Audience
**Who are we selling to?**
- Researchers, writers, and students managing large amounts of information.
- Professionals (Legal, Medical, Engineering) with niche knowledge bases.
- Knowledge workers looking for an AI that "actually remembers" their data.

## 3. Primary Distribution Channels
- **Twitter/X:** Engaging with the "Personal Knowledge Management" (PKM) and productivity community.
- **Reddit:** Focused subreddits like r/productivity, r/obsidian, and r/notion.
- **LinkedIn:** Positioning the tool as a way to build a "Second Brain" for career growth.
- **Product Hunt:** Launching to the productivity tool enthusiast crowd.

## 4. Content Strategy
- **"Your AI Second Brain":** Demos showing the tool recalling obscure facts from a user's uploaded data.
- **Workflow Connectors:** Showing how Astra AI syncs with Notion, Obsidian, and browser bookmarks.
- **Personal AI Demos:** "Watch me ask my AI about that research paper I read 3 months ago."
- **Knowledge Management Hacks:** General tips on organizing digital information.

## 5. Growth Hack / Viral Loop
**The "Public Claw" Loop:**
Allow users to create and share "Public Claws" (curated AI knowledge bases on specific topics like "AI Safety" or "Web3 Dev"). When these are shared on social media, they act as high-value lead magnets that showcase the product's utility to new users.
