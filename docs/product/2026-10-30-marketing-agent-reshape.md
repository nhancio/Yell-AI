# Astra: marketing agent for founders

Date: 30 October 2026.
Status: proposed product direction. Website versus working-product implementation scope is awaiting the user's selection. This brief does not claim the proposed capabilities are already available.

## Positioning

Astra is a marketing agent for founders that turns a product brief into a coherent campaign, using the founder's brand, audience, and approved messaging.

First audience: early-stage founders who handle their own product launches and weekly social content.

Working promise: **Send your product link. Get a launch campaign in your brand.**

Supporting copy: Research, write, create images and videos, and refine everything in one conversation.

Retain Astra as the working name until the user chooses a replacement. Preserve the warm, approachable visual language requested earlier, while making campaign assets the main visual proof of the product.

## First repeatable workflow

1. The founder supplies a product URL, a brief, and optionally brand assets.
2. Astra proposes an editable brand profile: product, audience, positioning, tone, colors, logo, and approved factual claims.
3. The founder chooses an outcome, starting with a feature/product launch or a week of founder content.
4. Astra prepares a compact campaign plan and identifies any missing information.
5. Astra produces drafts and creative assets using connected generation/editing services.
6. The founder requests revisions and approves individual versions.
7. Astra packages the approved copy and downloadable assets with a publishing checklist.

Initial publishing is an export workflow. Direct posting, account changes, advertising spend, and campaign activation are separate capabilities that require actual integrations and explicit user approval.

## Initial capabilities

| Area | First useful scope |
|---|---|
| Research | Source-linked competitor, positioning, and campaign research |
| Writing | LinkedIn posts, launch emails, ad copy, headlines, and landing-page sections |
| Image creation | Branded campaign graphics and a small number of variations |
| Image editing | Background changes, object edits, cropping, resizing, and brand adjustments |
| Video creation | Short promotional sequences using supplied assets and generated clips |
| Video editing | Trimming, captions, voiceover, resizing, and demo-to-social cutdowns |
| Brand memory | Editable brand facts, approved claims, tone, visual assets, and preferences |
| Campaign organization | Briefs, asset versions, approvals, exports, and a publishing checklist |

Start with reliable integrations for a limited set of operations. Do not present advanced video editing, live competitor monitoring, performance optimization, or direct publishing as available until they work end to end.

## Example first campaign

Founder request: “We're launching a team dashboard next Friday. Make LinkedIn posts, three ad concepts, a launch email, and a short product video. Use our existing visual style.”

Deliverables:

- One editable campaign brief containing goal, audience, offer, channels, and call to action.
- Three distinct messaging angles for review.
- Channel-specific copy with source-backed product facts.
- Approved image variations and a short video where a generation service is configured.
- A package of chosen assets and a suggested publishing sequence.

No invented customer quotes, business results, product capabilities, or fabricated competitor facts. Creative suggestions and verified facts should remain distinguishable.

## Product surfaces

**Campaign workspace:** chat brief, campaign plan, asset previews, revisions, and export.

**Brand profile:** editable product/audience facts, brand voice, colors, logos, reference assets, and prohibited claims.

**Asset library:** original uploads, generated files, chosen versions, and export formats.

**Credits and account:** available usage, job-level estimates, generation history, account connections, and billing.

The web workspace is the core experience. Messaging can support intake, approvals, and notifications if the selected provider and channel permit this product's actual use case. Marketing-only language does not itself establish WhatsApp eligibility.

## Homepage reshape

Replace the personal-assistant promise with the founder-marketing promise. Lead with a realistic brief-to-campaign example rather than a personal WhatsApp conversation.

Suggested sections:

1. Hero: “Your product. Your brand. Your marketing, handled.”
2. Interactive campaign example, clearly marked as a preview if it uses sample results.
3. Four capabilities: research, writing, images, and video.
4. Brand memory and revision flow.
5. Three starting workflows: launch a feature, create a week of content, repurpose a demo.
6. Commercial model explanation without an unsupported price or unlimited-generation promise.
7. FAQs and a clear entry into the campaign experience.

Remove promotion of travel help, personal finance, household reminders, job hunting, and the advertiser-wallet product from the main marketing journey. Existing routes and data should not be deleted merely to change positioning.

Use accurate capability states: sample preview, available, needs a connection, or planned. A browser-only preview must not pretend a paid render, web research task, or social post happened.

## Commercial model hypothesis

Subscription covers the workspace, brand profiles, campaign organization, and a defined writing/research allowance. Image and video operations use metered credits, with an estimate shown before starting a job.

Specify how failures, retries, revisions, model choices, duration, and resolution affect credits. Advertising spend is separate. Final prices and allowances follow measured provider costs and early customer usage; the old ₹99 personal-assistant plan is not the default marketing plan.

## Working-product requirements

The existing hosted application does not yet provide the proposed media workflows. Production work would need:

- A verified user-owned brand and campaign model.
- Actual research, writing, image, and video service execution.
- Durable jobs with progress, failure recovery, cancellation, and clear results.
- Asset storage and versioned revisions.
- Job-level usage accounting tied to real provider responses.
- Explicit approval for publishing and spend, when those capabilities are introduced.

Changing prompts and homepage copy alone would not deliver these outcomes. The proposed reshape should keep presentation work and proven product capabilities honest.

## Success criteria

For a website and preview: every primary journey concerns founder marketing; preview results are clearly labeled; no old unlimited-use or general-assistant promises remain in that journey; desktop and mobile layouts work.

For a working first release: a founder can complete one real campaign from brief to exported assets, edit an output, retain an approved version, see the actual usage charged, and recover from a failed generation without duplicate charges. Paid-customer testing should measure time to an approved campaign, accepted asset rate, cost per campaign, repeat usage, and willingness to pay.
